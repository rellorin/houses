Blakeslee is an unincorporated crossroads community in Tobyhanna Township. Fawn Ridge Estates sits northwest of the PA-115/PA-940 junction, so everyday life is quiet and wooded while shopping and services spread across the western Pocono road network.

General vibe

Wooded, quiet and low-density, with private roads, wells and an HOA-managed sewer arrangement. The property feels secluded, but Blakeslee grocery and highway access are only a short drive away.
