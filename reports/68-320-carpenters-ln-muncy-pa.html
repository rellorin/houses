What the area feels like

The niche

The house sits in a rural-agricultural pocket outside Muncy Borough, with fields, mature trees, Carpenter’s Run and a private pond. Downtown Muncy, the schools, grocery shopping and the former Lycoming Mall retail corridor are all practical by car. The historical character is exceptional, but the property behaves more like a small farmstead than a conventional suburban home.

Community rhythm

Muncy supports family-friendly Fourth Friday events downtown, Muncy Historical Society programs and walking tours, library activities, school athletics and arts, and regional events at the Lycoming County Fairgrounds. Williamsport adds museums, festivals and larger cultural programming.
