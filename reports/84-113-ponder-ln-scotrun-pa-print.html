General vibe

Wooded, low-density and quiet at home, but far from isolated. Schools and several essentials are unusually close; walking to them is still unrealistic.

Local niche

A practical Pocono base: Tannersville handles grocery and early learning, Mount Pocono adds retail and fitness, and Stroudsburg supplies the broadest downtown and activity network.

Events families notice

  • Fishing Derby: A spring family fishing day built around youth participation and outdoor recreation.
  • Touch-A-Truck: Children can explore municipal, emergency and working vehicles up close.
  • Concerts + movies: Seasonal outdoor music and movie nights create an easy local evening out.
  • Food Truck Festival: A multi-day September gathering with food vendors and community entertainment.
  • Spooky Walk + Trunk or Treat: Two fall traditions aimed squarely at families with younger children.
  • Tree Lighting + Mistletoe Market: The township closes the year with lights, vendors and a holiday-market atmosphere.
