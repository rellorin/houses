This address sits at the quiet western edge of Exeter Township near the Reiffton school campus. It feels wooded and secluded at home, but the Perkiomen Avenue commercial corridor and central Reading are both only minutes away.

Exeter Township counted 25,466 residents in the 2020 Census across 24.23 square miles of land. This western pocket combines mature residential streets, wooded slopes and conservation land with immediate access to US 422 and Reading-area services.

Daily feel

A wooded retreat hidden inside a highly practical suburban location. Schools and errands are close enough for short drives, while the 1.78-acre site and conserved land behind it create a quieter atmosphere than the mileage suggests.

The local niche

Exeter school access with Reading-city amenities close at hand. It suits a family that wants mature trees, a large character home and privacy without giving up fast retail, fitness, arts or youth-program access.

What to notice

The listing reports 4 bedrooms, 2.5 baths and 4,233 square feet, while summarized public records show 3 bedrooms, 1 bath and 3,102 square feet. Verify additions, permits, finished-basement area, the 1902 structure, roof/skylights, fireplaces, drainage and the precise conservation-land boundary.
