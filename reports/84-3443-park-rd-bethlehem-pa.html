What the area feels like

The niche

Bethlehem Township provides a suburban, car-oriented setting just east of the city, with hospitals, Route 33, I-78 and the Bethlehem/Easton retail corridors close by. The parcel’s mature trees and private street create a more secluded feel than most nearby neighborhoods, while historic Bethlehem supplies restaurants, festivals and cultural institutions.

Community rhythm

Bethlehem’s year is anchored by Musikfest, Celtic Classic, Christkindlmarkt, SteelStacks programming and seasonal Christmas City events. Township parks, the Bethlehem Township Community Center and nearby Easton add sports, markets and family programming.
