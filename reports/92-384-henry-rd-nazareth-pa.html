What it’s like here

This is a rural, historic pocket at the southern foot of Blue Mountain: acreage homes, quiet roads, wildlife and immediate access to Jacobsburg’s forests and trails, with Nazareth’s compact downtown and Route 33 retail corridor close enough to handle everyday needs.

Bushkill Township counted 8,482 residents in the 2020 Census across 24.95 square miles. It is a low-density rural-suburban township tied to Nazareth for schools, shopping and events, while Route 33 provides fast regional access.

Daily feel

Quiet, green and car-based, with a particularly strong outdoors orientation. The home overlooks the historic Henry homestead and sits opposite Jacobsburg parkland, while schools and core errands remain only a short drive away.

The local niche

Historic countryside with a music-and-nature identity. Jacobsburg preserves the Henry rifle-making landscape and offers year-round education and trails; nearby Nazareth is closely identified with Martin Guitar, a traditional downtown and strong school-community events.

What to notice

The MLS describes a four-bedroom, three-bath, 2,945-square-foot home built in 1890, but summarized public records show three bedrooms, two baths, 2,401 square feet and a 1920 build date. Renovations and a detached garage were started but not completed. Verify permits, plans, occupancy status, structure, roof, wiring, plumbing, heating, lead/asbestos, well quality and yield, septic documentation, drainage and the full cost to finish the project.
