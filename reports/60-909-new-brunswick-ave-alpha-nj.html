What the area feels like

The niche

Alpha is a compact Warren County borough tied closely to Phillipsburg, Easton and the broader Lehigh Valley. The address combines a large historic parcel with unusually quick access to the Route 22 retail corridor, I-78 and downtown Easton. It feels more mixed-use and connected than rural, with commercial properties and major shopping close by.

Community rhythm

Local life centers on Alpha Community Park, youth recreation, borough and fire-company events, Fall Fest and school activities. Easton adds the Crayola Experience, riverfront events, farmers markets, festivals, restaurants and arts programming within a short drive.
