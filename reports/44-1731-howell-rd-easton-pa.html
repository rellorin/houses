What the area feels like

The niche

Howell Road is in the rural Martins Creek side of Lower Mount Bethel Township, close to farms, wooded land and the Delaware River corridor. The setting is quiet and car-dependent rather than suburban: daily services are concentrated in Forks Township, Easton and across the river in Phillipsburg. Route 611 gives a direct north-south spine, but school, shopping and activities require planned drives.

Community rhythm

Lower Mount Bethel parks and recreation include the Centerfield School courts and playground, Riverton Ball Fields, the Jerry Brunetti Environmental Welcome Center and the five-mile John M. Mauser Nature Education Trail. The Meadows at Martins Creek adds fishing, walking, birding and picnicking, while Easton provides the farmers market, Heritage Day, State Theatre programming, festivals and the Crayola Experience.
