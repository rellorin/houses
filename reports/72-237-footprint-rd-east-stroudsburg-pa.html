Price Township is a low-density municipality of woods, rolling hills and quiet roads. The mailing identity is East Stroudsburg, but the property is outside the walkable borough and relies on PA 447 and Milford Road for daily services.

Daily feel

Wooded residential roads, larger lots and a quiet mountain setting define the immediate area. Expect wildlife, well and septic, minimal traffic noise and virtually no useful sidewalks.

The local niche

Rural privacy with better service access than the setting first suggests: groceries and early learning sit along Milford Road, while East Stroudsburg provides the broader family infrastructure.

Social radius

Price Township itself is civic and outdoors-oriented; most organized classes, school events, restaurants and festivals happen in East Stroudsburg, Stroudsburg and nearby Pocono communities.
