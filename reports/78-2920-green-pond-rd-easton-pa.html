What the area feels like

The niche

This is a mature Palmer Township residential corridor rather than a rural acreage setting. The parcel is close to Palmer Elementary and Easton Area High School, while Route 248, Route 33 and Route 22 make the broader Lehigh Valley practical. Most daily needs are a short drive, but Green Pond Road traffic and school-day activity should be tested at peak times.

Community rhythm

Palmer Township Community Weekend, Earth Day at Fairview Park, Charles Chrin Community Center programs and youth recreation provide a strong local calendar. Downtown Easton adds the farmers market, Heritage Day, festivals, State Theatre programming and the Crayola Experience.
