What the area feels like

The niche

This is suburban Stroud Township west of downtown Stroudsburg: wooded residential acreage with practical access to schools, I-80 and the Route 611 retail corridor. Downtown Stroudsburg adds restaurants, arts and community events, while the wider Poconos supply trails, creeks and four-season recreation.

Community rhythm

StroudFest brings music, vendors and family activities to Main Street; the producer-only Monroe Farmers Market runs at Courthouse Square; Sherman Theater hosts concerts and community programming; township parks offer local recreation; and the broader Pocono Mountains calendar adds fairs, outdoor programs and seasonal festivals.
