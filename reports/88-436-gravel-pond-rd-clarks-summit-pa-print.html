General vibe

South Abington is an established, higher-income Scranton suburb with leafy residential areas, larger lots and strong school identity. Clarks Summit supplies a compact town center, restaurants, shops and community institutions without sacrificing regional highway access.

Local niche

The Abingtons combine top regional schools with a real civic and cultural center. The Waverly Community House and downtown Clarks Summit provide more family programming than many comparable suburbs.

Events families notice

  • Festival of Ice: sculptures, carving, carriage rides and downtown family activities.
  • Fire and Ice: free s’mores, hot chocolate and live carving.
  • Seasonal family parties: Valentine’s, Bunny Breakfast and Halloween events.
  • Concerts and arts: summer/fall music, galleries and NEPA Film Festival.
  • Farmers markets: seasonal vendors and local food at The Comm.

Daily-life caveat

Destinations are close by car, but Gravel Pond Road is not a walkable main-street environment. Confirm sidewalks, crossings and student access before assuming the nearby high school can be reached safely on foot.
