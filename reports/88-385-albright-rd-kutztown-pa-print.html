General vibe

Quiet, pastoral and fully car-based. The house is surrounded by an agricultural landscape, but grocery, fitness and the closest private school are only a few minutes away; Kutztown’s schools and Main Street are generally around ten to twelve minutes.

Local niche

Pennsylvania Dutch farm country meeting a small university town. Kutztown supplies cafés, arts, sports and year-round campus energy, while Maxatawny retains the open-land, historic-building and roadside-farm character families often seek outside the Lehigh Valley suburbs.

Events families notice

  • Eastern Pennsylvania Homestead Festival: The new fairgrounds festival pays homage to the former Kutztown Folk Festival with traditional food, crafts, demonstrations and family programming.
  • Kutztown Fair: The long-running agricultural fair brings livestock, farm and craft exhibits, music, midway entertainment and community competitions to the fairgrounds.
  • Kutztown Day: A full community day in Kutztown Park combines music, youth activities, demonstrations, community groups and an evening fireworks finale.
  • Heritage Center farm festivals: Easter on the Farm, Heemet Fescht and Christmas on the Farm feature animals, music, folk arts, demonstrations, hayrides and children’s activities.
  • Fall Fest on Main: The Kutztown Community Partnership closes Main Street for a walkable fall festival with vendors, food, entertainment and family activities.
  • Historical Society traditions: The annual ice-cream social and Holiday Festival mix local history, live music, model trains and seasonal community gathering.
