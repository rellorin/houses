What the area feels like

The niche

Harding is a rural, low-density part of Exeter Township northwest of the Wyoming Valley. Kitchen Lane sits in a wooded, hilly setting with more privacy than the borough neighborhoods below, while Exeter, West Pittston, Pittston, Kingston and Wilkes-Barre provide the practical shopping and services. Daily life is car-dependent, and winter road conditions, slope, drainage and driveway access deserve an in-person check.

Community rhythm

The Harding Recreation Committee organizes local projects, meetings, raffles and an annual tree-lighting celebration. Greater Pittston and the Wyoming Valley add seasonal festivals, library and school events, community runs, parks, riverfront programming and minor-league sports within a regional drive.
