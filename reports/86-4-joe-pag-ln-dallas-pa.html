What the area feels like

The niche

Dallas is the service and community hub of the Back Mountain: wooded hills, established neighborhoods, farms and lakes, with Wilkes-Barre about a short regional drive away. The feel is suburban-rural rather than walkable urban.

Community rhythm

Back Mountain Farmers Market, Dallas Harvest Festival, Luzerne County Fair, library and makers’ workshops, youth sports, trail events and seasonal programs at nearby parks and Harveys Lake.
