General vibe

A wooded retreat hidden inside a highly practical suburban location. Schools and errands are close enough for short drives, while the 1.78-acre site and conserved land behind it create a quieter atmosphere than the mileage suggests.

Local niche

Exeter school access with Reading-city amenities close at hand. It suits a family that wants mature trees, a large character home and privacy without giving up fast retail, fitness, arts or youth-program access.

Events families notice

  • Exeter Fall Festival & Hayride: Live entertainment, hayrides, arts and crafts, food trucks and children’s activities at Daniel Boone Homestead.
  • Exeter youth sports seasons: Township organizations support baseball, softball, basketball, football, cheer, lacrosse, wrestling and track for children.
  • Reading Public Museum programs: Family exhibitions, planetarium shows, seasonal events and the museum arboretum expand the nearby cultural options.
  • Reading Royals hockey: Minor-league hockey at Santander Arena provides a recurring family sports outing through the fall and winter.
  • Downtown Reading events: Festivals, performances and community gatherings use Santander Arena, the performing-arts venues and downtown streets.
  • Hopewell Furnace programs: The nearby national historic site offers living-history demonstrations, seasonal programs and family visits in the historic iron landscape.

What to notice

The listing reports 4 bedrooms, 2.5 baths and 4,233 square feet, while summarized public records show 3 bedrooms, 1 bath and 3,102 square feet. Verify additions, permits, finished-basement area, the 1902 structure, roof/skylights, fireplaces, drainage and the precise conservation-land boundary.
