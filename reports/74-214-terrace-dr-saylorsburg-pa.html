What it’s like here

Chestnuthill is one of the West End’s larger townships. It feels rural off the main roads, but Brodheadsville’s US 209 corridor supplies supermarkets, services and family programs without a full Stroudsburg run.

Daily feel

Wooded lots, quiet side roads and mountain views sit close to a practical suburban-rural service strip. Expect a car, well and septic—not sidewalks or a walkable downtown.

The local niche

Poconos space without maximum isolation: calmer than Stroudsburg and more convenient than the farther-west rural townships, with US 209 as the family logistics spine.

Social radius

Brodheadsville covers groceries, dance, fitness and township programming; Kunkletown holds the K–5 campuses; Stroudsburg adds major retail, martial arts and dining.

Events in the local orbit

Community life leans outdoorsy, family-scale and seasonal rather than nightlife-driven.

Township summer programming

Chestnuthill runs summer camp and park concerts; its 2026 program included a six-week camp and a Big Valley Bluegrass evening.
