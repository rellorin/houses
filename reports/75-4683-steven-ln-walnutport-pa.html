What the area feels like

The niche

This Lehigh Township pocket is rural-suburban and car-dependent, with low-density homes, wooded hills and easy access to Walnutport, Slatington and PA-145. The cul-de-sac and quiet setting are appealing, but daily shopping and specialized activities are spread across Walnutport, Northampton, Whitehall and the wider Lehigh Valley.

Community rhythm

Walnutport and Slatington borough programming, Northern Lehigh recreation, Lehigh Township community events, Canal Street Park and nearby Blue Mountain and Delaware & Lehigh trail access shape the local rhythm. Larger festivals, museums and performances are available in Allentown and Bethlehem.
