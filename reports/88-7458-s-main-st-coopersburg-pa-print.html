General vibe

Upper Saucon is a growing Lehigh Valley bedroom community with a mix of rural landscapes, historic homes, established suburban neighborhoods and Route 309 commercial access. This house sits near Coopersburg’s small-town core but remains outside the borough.

Local niche

The area’s differentiator is the combination of high-performing Southern Lehigh schools and convenient access to Center Valley, Bethlehem, Allentown and Quakertown while retaining more space than denser suburban locations.

Events families notice

  • Coopersburg Community Day: free September festival, crafts, music, cars and extensive kids activities.
  • Halloween Parade: a signature community tradition.
  • Tree Lighting: annual holiday gathering.
  • Township parks: summer recreation, pavilions and community programming.
  • Living Memorial Park: sports and major community events.

Daily-life caveat

The location is convenient by car, but South Main Street traffic and limited pedestrian infrastructure make it less walkable than the short distances suggest.
