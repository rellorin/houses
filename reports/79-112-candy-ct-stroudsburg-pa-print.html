General vibe

Private, wooded and residential, with a long driveway and no through traffic on the cul-de-sac. The trade-off is total car dependence: even the closest practical destinations require a drive.

Local niche

A midpoint between the West End and the Stroudsburg/Tannersville orbit. It offers rural privacy without being isolated from full grocery shopping, youth activities, regional retail and Pocono outdoor recreation.

Events families notice

  • Chestnuthill summer camp + park programs: The township recreation program runs seasonal youth activities, summer camp and family programming at municipal parks.
  • Chestnuthill Township Park gatherings: The main park hosts concerts, recreation events, sports and community activities.
  • Quiet Valley farm programs: Living-history demonstrations, farm animals, seasonal festivals and educational programs create a distinctive nearby family outing.
  • Halloween Spooktacular: The township’s fall calendar includes a family-oriented Halloween celebration supported through its recreation program.
  • Pocono Mountains event calendar: Festivals, outdoor programs, fairs, races and seasonal celebrations rotate throughout the wider region.
  • Downtown Stroudsburg outings: Restaurants, shops, arts events, festivals and seasonal downtown activities are a practical regional outing.

What to notice

The MLS reports five bedrooms, three full baths and 3,335 finished square feet, including 587 square feet below grade; public records summarized by Redfin show three bedrooms, two baths and 2,748 square feet. Verify the finished-basement permits and legal bedroom egress, roof and deck ages, window-unit cooling capacity, mixed electric/propane heating costs, leased propane tank, well yield and water quality, septic records, drainage and the lack of a garage.
