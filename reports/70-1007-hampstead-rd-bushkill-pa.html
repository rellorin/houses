What it’s like here

Bushkill is an unincorporated Pike County community in sprawling Lehman Township. This address is specifically inside Saw Creek Estates, a gated four-season community next to the Delaware Water Gap recreation landscape.

The township counted 10,843 residents in 2020 across nearly 49 square miles. Census data show an unusually long 53-minute mean commute, reflecting its rural, exurban and New York–oriented geography.

Daily feel

Wooded, gated and recreation-heavy. The community supplies pools, courts, a fitness center, lakes, playgrounds and even a ski hill, while ordinary errands happen along Route 209 and Milford Road.

The local niche

A four-season resort community that also functions as a year-round neighborhood. It favors families who will use the amenities and accept longer drives for big-box retail and specialized programs.

What to notice

Redfin currently reports 3.65 acres while Zillow reports 0.45 acre; bathroom counts also differ. Verify the deed, parcel map, finished square footage, HOA fees, rental rules and amenity status before relying on listing summaries.
