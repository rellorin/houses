What it’s like here

Grassy Run sits in the rural-suburban center of Chestnuthill Township: wooded one-acre homes, winding roads and Pocono foothill scenery, with Brodheadsville handling most daily errands and Route 209 connecting the area to Stroudsburg.

Chestnuthill Township counted 16,723 residents in the 2020 Census across 37.40 square miles of land; the 2025 estimate was 16,555. It is one of Monroe County’s larger family townships, with more than 1,200 acres of parks and natural areas and a daily orbit centered on Brodheadsville.

Daily feel

Quiet, wooded and fully car-based. Most essential errands and the older-student schools are clustered along Route 209 only a few minutes away, while regional retail and specialty programs are farther east toward Stroudsburg.

The local niche

A practical West End of the Poconos location: more residential and year-round than a resort town, with township parks, youth programs and easy access to hiking, farms and the broader Pocono events calendar.

What to notice

The listing describes a 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath, 3,019-square-foot home with a separate-entry in-law apartment containing its own kitchen, living room, bedroom and bath. Verify zoning and permits for the second living unit and kitchen, legal egress, septic design capacity, well quality and yield, basement moisture, deck condition, HVAC warranties, any HOA obligations and the exact current tax bill.
