General vibe

Quiet, wooded and decidedly car-based. The two-acre lot and screened porch create a retreat-like setting, while Target, Weis, Montessori, gymnastics, fitness and martial arts remain close enough for ordinary family routines.

Local niche

Pocono privacy without deep-rural isolation. Bartonsville and Tannersville handle daily errands and children’s programs; Stroudsburg adds a walkable downtown, arts and dining; Camelback and state-park recreation are approximately fifteen minutes away.

Events families notice

  • Pocono Township Fishing Derby: A spring family fishing event organized through the township recreation calendar.
  • Touch-A-Truck: Children can explore construction, emergency and specialty vehicles at one of the township’s largest family events.
  • Concerts + movies in the park: Outdoor concerts and movie nights recur through the summer at township parks.
  • Pocono Township Food Truck Festival: A multi-day September gathering with food vendors and community activities.
  • Spooky Walk + Trunk or Treat: Two family-focused Halloween events anchor the October calendar.
  • Tree Lighting + Mistletoe Market: The township closes the year with a community tree lighting and holiday market.

What to notice

The MLS reports four bedrooms, three full and two half baths, 3,078 square feet above grade plus 1,436 finished below grade, a private well and on-site septic. Verify lower-level permits and egress, roof-warranty transfer, HVAC age, propane-tank ownership and costs, well yield and water quality, septic records, radon, pond and site drainage, HOA documents and the separate furnishings package.
