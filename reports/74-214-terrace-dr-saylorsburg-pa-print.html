Wooded Poconos living with a useful West End service corridor

The property is in Chestnuthill Township, Monroe County. Saylorsburg is the mailing identity, while Brodheadsville and US 209 provide the practical everyday center.

Niche: Academics B− · Diversity B+ · Teachers C+ · Resources D

The 2020 Census counted 16,723 residents; the July 2025 estimate is 16,555. About 20% of residents are under 18.

The niche

Poconos space without maximum isolation: quieter than Stroudsburg and more convenient than the farther-west rural townships, with US 209 as the family logistics spine.
