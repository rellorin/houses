Place profile · Cumru Township & Shillington

Quiet cul-de-sac, direct park access

The mailing address says Reading, but the home is in Cumru Township’s Overbrook neighborhood, immediately south of Shillington. This is an established suburban pocket with mature trees, quick services and a direct physical connection to Shillington Park.
