General vibe

Quiet, leafy and car-based. The cul-de-sac and 1.33-acre lot create privacy, while the elementary school and Montessori option are close enough to make early-childhood routines unusually practical for a rural setting.

Local niche

Country living inside the Governor Mifflin orbit. Mohnton and Shillington provide the Y, schools and community traditions; Reading and Wyomissing add major retail, specialized children’s activities and broader entertainment.

Events families notice

  • Governor Mifflin Community Days: A week-long summer fair with more than forty-five food and game stands, amusement rides, live entertainment and fireworks.
  • Community Days parade: The anniversary parade welcomes families, community groups, decorated bicycles, tractors and local organizations along the Shillington route.
  • Governor Mifflin Homecoming: School spirit week, athletics and family literacy activities bring the five-district community together each fall.
  • Nolde Forest family programs: Story walks, children’s STEAM sessions, guided hikes, nature journaling and mansion open houses run throughout the year.
  • Mustang arts + athletics: District concerts, performances, games and youth sports create a steady school-year calendar for families.
  • Brecknock community life: Township, fire-company and local civic notices reflect a small, volunteer-oriented rural community.

What to notice

The listing describes a four-bedroom, 2.5-bath traditional home with 2,930 square feet, a full basement, attached two-car garage, oil heat, central air, a private well and on-site septic. Verify the roof and mechanical ages, water quality and yield, septic condition, radon, drainage and any finished-basement work.
