What the area feels like

The niche

This is a car-dependent rural-suburban pocket outside Red Lion, with detached homes, open land and quick access to the borough and the PA-74 corridor. York provides the broader employment, retail and medical base, while the immediate setting is quieter and more residential.

Community rhythm

Family life centers on Red Lion and Windsor Township recreation, school activities, parks, seasonal borough events, farmers markets and the annual Red Lion Street Fair. York expands the calendar with Rail Trail outings, museums, fairs and regional sports and arts programming.
