The niche

The immediate setting feels private and car-dependent, while Moscow supplies schools and local services and Dickson City or Scranton provide the full retail and activity range.

Community rhythm

Moscow and the North Pocono region offer school athletics, library programming, township recreation and seasonal community events. Lackawanna County, Scranton, Montage Mountain and nearby state lands broaden weekends with festivals, museums, skiing, water parks, trails and performances.
