What the area feels like

The niche

This is a wooded, car-dependent Middle Smithfield Township setting near the Marshalls Creek commercial corridor rather than a downtown neighborhood. It balances country-road privacy and no HOA with practical access to Route 209, I-80, East Stroudsburg, Delaware Water Gap and the recreation base of the Poconos.

Community rhythm

Family life can draw from township parks and seasonal programs, Marshalls Creek Fire Company events, East Stroudsburg and Stroudsburg community calendars, Monroe Farmers Market, StroudFest, Sherman Theater programming, Delaware Water Gap recreation and the broader Pocono Mountains festival and outdoor calendar.

Pros

  • 3,286 reported finished square feet and five bedrooms provide substantial family and work-from-home flexibility
  • A first-floor bedroom can support guests or multigenerational use
  • The $410,000 asking price leaves meaningful headroom under the $600,000 search ceiling
  • Price Chopper, Montessori-style preschool and a family BJJ academy are short local drives
  • No HOA, a two-car attached garage, fenced area and finished basement add practical utility
