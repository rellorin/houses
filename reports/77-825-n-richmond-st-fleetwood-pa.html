What the area feels like

The niche

Fleetwood is a compact Berks County borough surrounded by farms and preserved land. This address is unusually close to the district campus, parks, library and borough businesses, while Reading, Kutztown and the Route 222 corridor provide the larger retail and activity base. The two-acre setting feels greener and more private than a typical in-town parcel.

Community rhythm

Fleetwood’s calendar centers on school activities, borough concerts and park programs, community celebrations, fire-company events and nearby Kutztown and Reading festivals. The Kutztown Folk Festival, Berks County fairs and Reading-area arts and sports broaden the weekend options.
