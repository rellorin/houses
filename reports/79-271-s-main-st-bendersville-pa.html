What the area feels like

The niche

Bendersville is a tiny Adams County borough surrounded by orchards, farms and South Mountain scenery. Daily life is quiet and car-based; Biglerville covers basics and Gettysburg supplies larger shopping, health care, activities and tourism-driven events.

Community rhythm

Bendersville Community Fire Department carnival, National Apple Harvest Festival, Adams County Farmers Market, Gettysburg festivals and parades, orchard events, youth sports and nearby state-forest recreation.
