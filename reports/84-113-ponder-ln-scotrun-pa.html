What it’s like here

Pocono Township sits in the center of Monroe County: resort-country scenery, major visitor attractions and everyday services strung along Routes 611, 715 and 940.

The township had 10,884 residents in the 2020 Census and describes itself as the heart of Monroe County. Residential pockets sit close to regional retail, resorts and Interstate 80.

Daily feel

Wooded, low-density and quiet at home, but far from isolated. Schools and several essentials are unusually close; walking to them is still unrealistic.

The local niche

A practical Pocono base: Tannersville handles grocery and early learning, Mount Pocono adds retail and fitness, and Stroudsburg supplies the broadest downtown and activity network.

What to notice

The active listing is a large 1989 home with a walk-out lower level, hot tub and two garages. Verify finished square footage, HOA rules, septic/well, older systems and basement moisture.
