General vibe

Quiet and residential with a large private yard, mature trees and mountain-valley surroundings. The house is close to Hughesville schools and town events, but almost every errand still requires a car.

Local niche

A practical small-town base for families who want acreage without being far from schools. Hughesville’s identity is tied to East Lycoming schools, agriculture and the long-running Lycoming County Fair, with bigger shopping and employment centered around Muncy, Montoursville and Williamsport.

Events families notice

  • Lycoming County Fair: Hughesville hosts the county’s historic mid-July agricultural fair with livestock, rides, food, demonstrations, tractor pulls and grandstand entertainment.
  • Hughesville Area Public Library: The library maintains an active calendar with children’s programs, clubs, homeschool activities and community learning events.
  • Spartan athletics and arts: East Lycoming’s school calendar brings sports, concerts, theater and family activities throughout the academic year.
  • Borough calendar: Municipal meetings, seasonal notices and local events are collected on Hughesville Borough’s public calendar.
  • Fairgrounds off-season events: The fairgrounds also hosts horse shows, flea markets, vehicle events and other gatherings outside the July fair window.
  • Lycoming County events: Williamsport and the broader county add festivals, museums, holiday markets, outdoor programs and family entertainment.

What to notice

The current MLS reports 3,259 finished square feet—2,628 above grade plus 631 below grade—while public-record summaries report 2,966. Confirm permits and what is included. Public water is reported; sewer/septic and official FEMA flood-zone status were not verified and need direct diligence.
