Place profile · Delaware Water Gap Borough

A tiny trail town with an outsized arts identity

The property is inside Delaware Water Gap Borough, not merely the wider ZIP code: a historic village where the Appalachian Trail, Delaware River, jazz history, inns and cafés meet at the eastern edge of the Poconos.

B+
Stroudsburg Area district grade
Niche: Academics B · Diversity A · Teachers B+ · College Prep A− · Activities A

The 2020 Census counted only 675 residents. The 2024 ACS reports median household income of $95,313 and 42.6% of adults holding a bachelor’s degree or higher.

The niche

An Appalachian Trail gateway with a serious jazz tradition—one of the rare small towns where outdoors, history and live music are part of the everyday identity.

What the area feels like

At home1.17 acres, five bedrooms, four finished levels, detached two-car garage, public water and public sewer.
NearbyMain Street offers restaurants, cafés, music and village life; the national recreation area and trailheads sit close by.
Broader orbitStroudsburg and East Stroudsburg add schools, supermarkets, classes, healthcare and major retail in roughly 10–15 minutes.

Events and family life

COTA Jazz Festival

A major September music-and-arts weekend rooted in the village since 1978.

Gazebo concerts

Free Sunday-evening music on the Church of the Mountain lawn from June through August.

Hiker dinners

Weekly summer meals for Appalachian Trail hikers reinforce the trail-community culture.

National park programs

Hiking, paddling, fishing, history and ranger-led exploration along the river corridor.

Daily reality

You can walk to some village destinations and outdoor recreation, but Redfin still classifies the address as car-required for most errands. I-80 access is useful and also a noise consideration.
