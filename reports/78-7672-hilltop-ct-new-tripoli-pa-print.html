General vibe

Quiet country-suburban living on a low-traffic cul-de-sac. The home has broad hill and valley views, while the school campus and New Tripoli services remain close enough for short drives. Walking for errands is not realistic.

Local niche

Northwestern Lehigh school access with a rural Lehigh Valley lifestyle. The area suits families who prioritize space, calm roads, local schools and outdoor recreation over immediate big-box retail and dense activity choices.

Events families notice

  • Pioneer Apple Fest: The Lynn-Heidelberg Historical Society fills Ontelaunee Park with pioneer demonstrations, traditional crafts, food and local history.
  • New Tripoli Fire Company events: The volunteer fire company hosts fundraisers, open houses, seasonal activities and community meetings.
  • Northwestern Lehigh school calendar: Athletics, concerts, theater, family nights and district programs provide the area’s most consistent school-year calendar.
  • Ontelaunee Park gatherings: The township park’s pavilions, historic buildings and open grounds host reunions, civic events and community programs.
  • Leaser Lake recreation: The 540-acre county and state recreation area supports fishing, paddling, trails, picnics and nature outings close to home.
  • Allemaengel regional celebrations: Historical societies in Lynn-Heidelberg, Albany and Weisenberg organize Pennsylvania German heritage events in the surrounding rural communities.

What to notice

The active listing reports 6 bedrooms, 2 baths and 2,967 finished square feet, while summarized public records report 5 bedrooms and 2,890 square feet. Verify the measured living area, bedroom egress, lower-level finish, well and septic condition, heat-pump capacity, window-unit cooling, detached garage/workshop, fireplace and any permits for later alterations.

Pros, trade-offs & methodology

What works well

  • All three Northwestern Lehigh public-school campuses are approximately five to seven minutes away.
  • The middle school currently rates 7/10 and the high school 8/10.
  • Next Level Dance Academy and 24-hour Ambition Gym share a convenient local Route 309 location around seven minutes from home.
  • Blue Mountain Christian Day School offers a nearby small Mennonite elementary/middle option.
  • The acre lot, cul-de-sac setting, detached garage/workshop and hill-and-valley views provide uncommon space and quiet.

What to plan around

  • A proper full-line supermarket is about fourteen minutes away; Target and Walmart are roughly twenty-two to twenty-four minutes.
  • Authentic Montessori for children under five requires a planned trip to Breinigsville.
  • Dedicated gymnastics and a verified academy combining BJJ and Muay Thai are approximately twenty-eight to thirty minutes away in Allentown.
  • The property is effectively non-walkable for everyday errands.
