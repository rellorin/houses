What the area feels like

The niche

Mohrsville is a small, rural Berks County community in Centre Township, with farmland, wooded hills and spread-out neighborhoods. The 2020 census counted 469 residents in the Mohrsville CDP. Daily life is quiet and car-dependent; Leesport and Route 61 handle basics, while Reading and Hamburg supply broader shopping and activities.

Community rhythm

Nearby community life centers on the Leesport Farmers Market, Berks County Heritage Center programs, township and fire-company events, the Kutztown Folk Festival, Hamburg’s seasonal festivals, youth sports and outdoor recreation along the Schuylkill River and Blue Marsh Lake.
