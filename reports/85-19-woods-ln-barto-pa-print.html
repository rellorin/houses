The general vibe

More countryside than town center: wooded and agricultural roads, detached homes, quiet nights and little walkability. This is a calm, private, home-centered setting for households that value space and nature. The trade-off is that errands, classes and social plans almost always begin with a car trip.

Where community life happens

Close to homeSchools, churches and fire-company gatherings provide the most local social fabric. Events are smaller and less centralized than in a traditional town center.
