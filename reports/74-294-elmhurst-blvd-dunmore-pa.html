What the area feels like

The niche

The property sits in a quiet, low-density pocket near Lake Scranton while remaining close to Dunmore and Scranton services. It feels more residential and wooded than central-city blocks, but grocery, healthcare, schools, restaurants, parks and the I-81/I-84 corridor are nearby. The location is practical for everyday errands, though the house itself is a very large historic property rather than an acreage retreat.

Community rhythm

Dunmore and greater Scranton offer borough concerts, Bucktown Musicfest, school and church events, Nay Aug Park programming, museums, theaters, festivals and minor-league sports. Lake Scranton and nearby reservoir trails support close-to-home walking, while Lackawanna State Park and the broader Pocono region expand weekend recreation.
