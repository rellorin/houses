The useful trade-offs, stripped of real-estate gloss.

What works well

  • ✓Mifflin Park Elementary is about a 10-minute routed drive and currently carries a 6/10 GreatSchools rating.
  • ✓La Salle Academy and Montessori Country Day create practical private and early-learning alternatives.
  • ✓Dance, gymnastics, an adult gym, a family YMCA, BJJ and Muay Thai are all within about 11–15 routed minutes.

What to plan around

  • –Imperial Drive is car-dependent; the report assigns only 10/100 for walkability despite nearby suburban services.
  • –A proper grocery run is about 15 minutes, while Walmart and Target are roughly 17–19 minutes away without live traffic.
  • –The closest Montessori choice found starts at age 2 years 10 months; families needing infant/toddler care must look beyond this Montessori option.
