What the area feels like

The niche

The house sits on an elevated, mature parcel in compact West Easton, close to Wilson schools and the amenities of Easton, Wilson Borough and Palmer Township. This is a convenient in-town setting rather than a rural acreage property: daily errands, Route 22 and downtown Easton are practical, while nearby streets, neighboring homes and traffic should be evaluated at school and commute times.

Community rhythm

Downtown Easton supplies the Easton Farmers’ Market, Heritage Day, PA Bacon Fest, State Theatre programming and the Crayola Experience. West Easton Borough and nearby Wilson Borough add parks, recreation and seasonal community programming; verify the current municipal calendars.
