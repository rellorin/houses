What the area feels like

The niche

The home sits in rural Greenfield Township north of the Mid Valley and south of Carbondale, with a level, private setting and little walkability. School and township recreation are close; grocery and fitness are manageable, while big-box shopping and specialty activities require trips toward Dickson City, Olyphant or Scranton.

Community rhythm

Greenfield Township Community Park, Merli-Sarnoski County Park, Lakeland youth sports, Greenfield Township Historical Society programs and seasonal events in Carbondale, Jermyn and Mayfield shape family life. Dickson City and Scranton add museums, performances and larger festivals.
