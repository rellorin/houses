General vibe

Wooded, gated and recreation-heavy. The community supplies pools, courts, a fitness center, lakes, playgrounds and even a ski hill, while ordinary errands happen along Route 209 and Milford Road.

Local niche

A four-season resort community that also functions as a year-round neighborhood. It favors families who will use the amenities and accept longer drives for big-box retail and specialized programs.

Events families notice

  • Saw Creek WinterFest: Ski and sled races, games, a firepit, treats, vendors and family activities in February.
  • Saw Creek Community Day: A mid-June resident gathering with games, music, entertainment and a community cookout.
  • Summer pool parties: Seasonal parties use the community’s pools, barbecue areas and Tiki Bar.
  • Fall Festival + Haunted Trail: Pumpkin painting, hayrides, trunk-or-treat, costumes and a spooky trail around Halloween.
  • Country at the Creek: A family-friendly day of live country music, food and community groups at Lehman Township Park.
  • Lehman Park traditions: The township park hosts concerts, a children’s fishing derby, community programming and seasonal events.

What to notice

Redfin currently reports 3.65 acres while Zillow reports 0.45 acre; bathroom counts also differ. Verify the deed, parcel map, finished square footage, HOA fees, rental rules and amenity status before relying on listing summaries.
