What the area feels like

The niche

This edge-of-borough location combines a quiet, landscaped setting with unusually quick access to downtown Danville, Geisinger Medical Center and daily services. The area is car-dependent but compact by rural central-Pennsylvania standards, with the Susquehanna River, historic Mill Street and Bloomsburg nearby.

Community rhythm

Danville supports a Saturday Growers’ Market, Spring Fling, the Fall Arts & Crafts Festival, Third Thursdays, River Front Park programming and seasonal downtown events. Sunnybrook Park and Pool and the wider Columbia-Montour region add family recreation.
