Place profile · Jefferson Township & Bernville

Rural privacy with a small-town service spine

Bernville is the mailing label, but the home is in Jefferson Township, southwest of the borough. The immediate setting is low-density, wooded and agricultural; Route 183 connects the household to school, groceries, Leesport and Reading.

The niche

A golf-course-edge acreage property that feels secluded while remaining about eight minutes from a full supermarket. This is appealing for space and quiet, less so for spontaneous classes or walkable social life.

Koziar’s Christmas Village

About four minutes away. Its 79th season opens November 7, 2026; nightly operation begins at Thanksgiving through January 2.

Beach days, boating, fishing, trails, picnics and seasonal recreation in the nearby lake corridor.

Library & borough programs

Storytimes, children’s programming and smaller civic events provide local indoor gathering points.

Seasonal caution
