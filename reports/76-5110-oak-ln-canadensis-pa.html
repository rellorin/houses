What the area feels like

The niche

This is a quiet, heavily wooded Price Township setting in The Hamlet, a private residential community near Canadensis and Mountainhome. Daily life is rural and car-dependent, but the association brings unusually strong on-site recreation: indoor and outdoor pools, a clubhouse, fitness center, trails and tennis. Lewis’ Supermarket covers nearby groceries, while Mount Pocono, Tannersville and Stroudsburg provide the larger retail and activity network.

Community rhythm

Barrett Township and the Canadensis/Mountainhome area offer seasonal community programs, library activities and local fire-company events. Nearby Buck Hill–Skytop and the wider Pocono Mountains add outdoor recreation, festivals and resort programming; Stroudsburg supplies the larger farmers-market, theater and downtown-event calendar.
