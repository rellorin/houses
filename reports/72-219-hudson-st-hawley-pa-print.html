Place profile · Hawley Borough

Small-town charm with a lake-region pulse

Hawley is a compact borough on the Lackawaxen River and a gateway to Lake Wallenpaupack. The permanent population is small, but tourism supports more restaurants, lodging, events and independent businesses than the number alone suggests.

B+
Niche overall grade
Schools B+ · Safety A · Families B+ · Outdoors A− · Commute A−

Niche reports a recent population estimate of 925; the 2020 Census counted 1,229. The borough covers roughly 0.6 square mile and has a dense small-town street pattern.

The niche

A historic in-town estate that can walk to park, river, dining and community life—a rare contrast to the car-dependent lake developments surrounding Hawley.
