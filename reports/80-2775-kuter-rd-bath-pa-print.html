General vibe

Rural-residential and spacious rather than isolated. Moore Township’s preserved-farm landscape and low-density roads give the home a country feel, while Bath and Nazareth keep ordinary services within reach.

Local niche

Bath is the compact historic hometown; Nazareth is the more active restaurant, retail and activity hub. Allentown and Bethlehem expand the job, cultural and martial-arts options farther south.

Events families notice

  • Community Days: bands, vendors, community groups and fireworks.
  • Summer Band Nights: free, family-friendly township concerts.
  • Bath Farmers Market: a limited 2026 late-summer pilot market.
  • Movies in the park: outdoor family nights at Friendship Stage.
  • Bath concerts: seasonal live music at the park stage.
  • Old Home Weekend: a September borough tradition.

Daily-life caveat

The nearby elementary and sports complex are valuable, but there is no walk-to-errands pattern. Most shopping and organized activities require a 13–18 minute drive.
