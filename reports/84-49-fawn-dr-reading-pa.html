What the area feels like

The niche

Angelica Woods is a wooded, low-density pocket of Cumru Township just south of Reading. The setting feels secluded, but Route 10 and I-176 are close enough for practical access to Shillington, Wyomissing and the larger Reading-area shopping and employment base. Daily errands and activities still require a car.

Community rhythm

Nearby community life includes Governor Mifflin Community Days, Reading Public Museum programming, Nolde Forest walks and education events, Berks County Heritage Center activities, Reading-area festivals and youth programs through the Mifflin Area YMCA.
